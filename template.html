ABOUT

Hi, I’m Trinh.

I develop machine learning methods for computational pathology, with a focus on learning useful representations from medical images and connecting visual evidence with diagnostic reasoning.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Mahmood Lab at Brigham and Women’s Hospital, Harvard Medical School. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Korea University in 2026.

My research spans multimodal learning, self-supervised learning, and domain generalization, with applications in cancer grading and mitotic figure analysis.
